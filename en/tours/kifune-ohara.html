Leave central Kyoto behind and discover a quieter side of the city — shaped by mountain water, cedar forests and centuries-old temples.

Travel north into the hills, where Kyoto feels slower, greener and far removed from the crowds. This is a day for riverside shrines, moss-covered gardens, village lanes and enough time to simply stay a little longer.

Two Places. One Quieter Kyoto.

Kifune

Where Kyoto follows the water

Hidden in a narrow forested valley, Kifune has long been associated with water, prayer and the mountains. Walk beside the river, visit the shrine, try the famous water fortune, or simply stop for a coffee and enjoy the change of pace.

Why go?

To experience a side of Kyoto that feels intimate, elemental and deeply connected to nature.

Ohara

Temple gardens and country quiet

In the rural hills northeast of Kyoto, Ohara offers a very different rhythm from the city. At Sanzen-in, moss gardens, old halls and mountain scenery create one of Kyoto's most peaceful temple experiences. With more time, continue to places such as Hosen-in, wander through the village, stop for lunch or simply enjoy the countryside.

Why go?

For a softer, slower Kyoto — where temples, gardens and everyday village life sit naturally together.
